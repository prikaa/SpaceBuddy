Telescopes

Telescopes help scientists observe distant stars, galaxies, and other objects in space.

Meet the James Webb Space Telescope!

The James Webb Space Telescope observes the universe using infrared light. It helps scientists study distant galaxies and learn more about how stars and planets form.

SPACE TRANSPORTATION

Rockets

Rockets carry satellites, spacecraft, and scientific instruments from Earth into space.

How Do Rockets Help Us?

Rockets produce thrust to overcome Earth's gravity. They can carry spacecraft toward the Moon, other planets, and destinations beyond Earth.

Satellites

Satellites orbit Earth and help us with navigation, weather forecasting, communication, and observing our planet.

Satellites Are Everywhere!

Satellites help navigation systems estimate locations, allow communication over long distances, and collect information about Earth's weather and environment.
